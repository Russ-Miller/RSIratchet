## arxiv-2603-06064

Selected controlled planning comparison supports a cost-aware interactive-planning check, while proposed richer feedback remains untested.

- **Compare interactive planning with direct and symbolic baselines** (author-tested): For structured symbolic planning, compare a step-wise LLM tool loop with direct plan generation and a classical planner under the same deadline before assuming interaction improves planning.
