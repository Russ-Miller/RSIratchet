## Qualifications worth keeping

- Self-Harness uses its held-out split in the promotion gate. Its result is not
  a clean untouched final evaluation.
- Meta-Harness has controlled trace-access and held-out math comparisons, but
  its terminal harness is searched and evaluated on the same benchmark.
- EvoSafeHarness compares deployment-specific search against once-ported fixed
  defenses. The advantage is not proof against equally tuned baselines.
- ModularRSI reports successful transfer to different backbones. Model-specific
  tuning is something to test, not a universal obligation. Its main experiments
  sample 120 instances per relevant subset, rather than using all 2,000 tasks.
- FinSkillOps reports protected-gate effects, but public-benchmark gate confidence
  intervals include zero. Its monitored operational run changes routing and
  diagnosis too, and incomplete artifacts limit independent checking.

The workbench shows each proposal with a grounded short passage, conditions,
limitations and a validation experiment. Existing overlaps are refinements to
review, not proof that distinct rules are equivalent.
