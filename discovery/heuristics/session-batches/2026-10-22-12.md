## arxiv-2608-22610

Selected bank interventions show skill utility depends on composition and deployment distribution.

- **Audit new skills in the bank combinations where they will run** (author-tested): When admitting skills to a self-evolving memory agent, compare sampled coalition contributions with a whole-bank outcome gate rather than judging each skill as independently useful.
- **Re-audit a skill bank after domain transfer** (author-tested): When moving a learned memory-skill bank to a new domain, compare the full bank with selectively masked variants rather than assuming source-useful skills remain helpful.

## arxiv-2609-05933

Selected controlled evaluations support stronger pruning baselines and complete deployment-cost accounting.

- **Compare learned agent pruning with matched random pruning** (inferred): When evaluating multi-agent pruning, include a single-agent baseline and random pruning at the same structural budget, and measure aggregation overhead rather than counting removed edges alone.

## arxiv-2609-11677

Selected curation study and modification audit support failure attribution beyond mere recurrence, with explicit consistency regressions in the reduced-data result.

- **Distinguish recurring model mistakes from harness defects** (author-tested): Before turning recurring failures into permanent harness rules, aggregate them across tasks and check whether the proposed change repairs a runtime defect or merely forbids a legitimate action the model mishandles.
