# Codex session batch 06

Reviewed selected sections of 3 papers; 4 proposals. These are partial reviews, not independent validation. No separately billed inference calls.

## arxiv-2606-16710

Selected controlled misinformation results support skepticism prompts and exposure-matched baselines, while showing a late verifier can fail.

- **Test a source-unreliability warning before adding a verifier agent** (author-tested): When agents reason from potentially false retrieved or peer-produced context, compare a short source-skepticism instruction with the existing prompt before adding a late verification agent.
- **Match misinformation exposure when evaluating debate** (inferred): When evaluating whether debate resists misleading context, compare independent voting with the same number of informed and misinformed samples, rather than only a fully exposed single agent.

## arxiv-2606-21666

Selected statistical and cross-domain results support testing for communication-induced contamination, not claiming proven SSVP superiority over no synchronization.

- **Test whether shared-state broadcasts spread a wrong belief** (author-tested): Before broadcasting every agent state to all peers, inject plausible stale or incorrect shared facts and compare no synchronization, full broadcast and selective reconciliation.

## arxiv-2607-14166

Selected abstract, scope and model-free probe sections support testing side-effect barrier semantics. Full repair proof and deployment contract were not reviewed.

- **Probe cancellation and approval at the side-effect boundary** (inferred): Before relying on an agent framework approval, cancellation or timeout control, test whether parallel, replayed or already-running tools can still commit effects after the control fires.
