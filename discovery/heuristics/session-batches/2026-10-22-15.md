## arxiv-2609-17848

## arxiv-2608-23635

Selected intervention and composition tests support stage-aware diagnosis and cross-stage robustness testing.

- **Check upstream tool selection before patching argument reasoning** (author-tested): When a tool call has wrong arguments, test whether the agent first chose the wrong tool before adding parameter-binding reasoning instructions.
